# Privacy Policy for Mohrez Browser Assistant & Homepage

**Last updated:** November 2026

## 1. Overview
Mohrez Browser Assistant is a personal productivity suite designed to manage local bookmarks, custom quick-access links, autofill, and browser preferences.

## 2. Google User Data & Synchronization
When you choose to sign in with Google:
- **Google Drive Access**: The application only requests access to its own private application data folder (`drive.appdata`) to sync your bookmarks, settings, and encrypted configuration JSON backups.
- **Zero Third-Party Sharing**: Your Google user data, files, and personal credentials are never sold, transferred, or shared with any external third parties or advertisers.
- **Local Storage**: All synchronization operations occur locally or directly between your device and Google's official servers.

## 3. Data Retention and Deletion
You can disconnect your Google Account and delete all synchronized data at any time directly through the application settings or via Google Account Permissions.
